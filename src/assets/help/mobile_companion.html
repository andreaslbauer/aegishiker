Mobile Companion & Offline Field Mode

AegisHiker provides two mobile companion options that work without cellular reception at remote trailheads:

  1. Mobile Web Companion (PWA): Scan the QR code in the Phone Sync & Field Companion tab while on home Wi-Fi. Tap Cache Pack Offline to store the entire regional trail network and your composed routes in browser IndexedDB.
  2. Native iOS Companion (ios/AegisHiker): Swift/SwiftUI app using CoreLocation background GPS, CMAltimeter barometric elevation, and on-device SQLite trail condition filtering.

Trailhead Condition Filtering

Arrive at the trailhead and discover mud, ice, or a seasonal closure? Toggle condition chips (Avoid Muddy, Avoid Icy, Avoid Overgrown, Avoid Closed) to filter and re-evaluate trails 100% offline.
