Trail Composer & Elevation Profile

The Trail Composer lets you build custom loops, out-and-back hikes, or point-to-point traverses over the regional trail graph.

  • Click-to-Snap Waypoints: Left-click on the map canvas to add a waypoint. AegisHiker snaps your click to the nearest trail node and runs A* shortest-path routing across open trails.
  • Pacing Models: Switch between Naismith's Rule, Langmuir's Rule (steep descent adjustments), and Tobler's Hiking Function, adjusted for pack weight and surface condition.
  • "✓ I Hiked This Trail": Click this button to log your traced trail as a completed hike, scan your local AegisPhotoVoyager SQLite database for GPS-matched photos along the trail corridor, and generate a persisted interactive hike map.
