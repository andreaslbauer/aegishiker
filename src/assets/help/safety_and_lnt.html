Leave No Trace & Mountain Safety

The 10 Essentials

Always carry Navigation, Headlamp, Sun Protection, First Aid, Knife/Repair Kit, Fire Starter, Emergency Shelter, Extra Food, Extra Water, and Extra Layers—especially above treeline in alpine zones like the New Hampshire White Mountains or Rocky Mountain National Park.

Leave-Behind Emergency Contact Sheet

Use File → Export Leave-Behind Safety PDF before heading out to leave a printed or emailed copy of your planned route, waypoints, turnaround time, and overdue emergency contacts with a trusted person.
