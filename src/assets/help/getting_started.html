Getting Started with AegisHiker

AegisHiker is a 100% local, privacy-first hybrid desktop and mobile hiking planner, trail composer, field GPS companion, and interactive hike replay studio.

Core Workflow

  1. Explore Destinations: Browse 7 bundled iconic regional trail packs (including New Hampshire White Mountains, Yosemite Valley, Acadia, Shenandoah, Rocky Mountain Bear Lake, Zion Canyon, and Mount Rainier Paradise) or download custom regions via Overpass + Open-Elevation. The Hikes tab lists the hikes you already built in the region; pick one to open it in the Trail Composer.
  2. AI Trail Brainstormer: Answer a guided trail interview (party profile, target distance, elevation cap, loop preference, dogs/kids) to receive ranked route recommendations.
  3. Interactive Trail Composer: Click waypoints on the vector topographic map to snap routes along trail junctions with live elevation profiles and Naismith/Langmuir/Tobler pacing estimates.
  4. "I Hiked This Trail" & Re-experience: Record a traced hike, automatically discover geotagged photos from AegisPhotoVoyager along your trail corridor, and replay your hike with an interactive map and photo timeline.
  5. Mobile Companion & Offline Field Mode: Pair your phone over local Wi-Fi QR code to cache regional packs in IndexedDB, filter trail conditions at the trailhead without cell reception, and record live GPS breadcrumbs.

After a Hike: Log It, Pick Your Photos, Relive It, Share It

  1. Find the trailhead: In Explore Regions, type the parking lot or trailhead in the search box (e.g. “Crow Hill Pond Parking”). The trails there are listed; click Start Hike Here.
  2. Build the hike you walked: In the Trail Composer, add the trails segment by segment as you turned onto them, then Save Hike. Answer Yes to log it as hiked.
  3. Log it: I Hiked This Trail finds your photos in AegisPhotoVoyager along the route. Add who came along and the weather (Look Up Weather fetches it from Open-Meteo), then Review Photos… to leave out the ones you don't want. Photo files are never changed.
  4. Play it back: Re-experience Hike → Play 3D Replay. The light follows the time of day: the sun stands where it did as you walked (golden hour, dusk and night included), from the hike's start, end and photo times. Review Photos… and Details… work here too for saved hikes.
  5. Make a website: Re-experience Hike → Create Website… writes a folder with a summary page, Play the Hike, a photo gallery (each photo on the trail map) and an interactive map with the elevation profile. Optionally leave out photos with people in them, and password protect it (everything is encrypted; protected sites need https://), then publish it with Upload Website over FTP/FTPS. The FTP password is never saved.
